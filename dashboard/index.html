Where a change goes

  1. AI playgroundClaude’s own copy. It tries ideas there on its own, with nobody waiting. Most stay there.
  2. DevYour workbench. Only the changes you picked, to try together and fix up.
  3. UATThe rehearsal. People check it works for them before it ships.
  4. ProdThe live app everyone uses.
Why a playground before dev?

Dev should only hold work you chose. Claude makes new ideas every hour, some great and some not, and pouring them all into dev would bury your own work and leave you sorting out which change broke what. The playground keeps every experiment in a copy of its own, so you can look at each one with before and after pictures and throw any away at no cost. Nothing leaves it until you cherry-pick it, so every change in dev is one you meant to make.
